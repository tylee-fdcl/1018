### Aerospace Engineer (7th Grade)

#### A.	Choose a job or profession that you may want to have in about 20 years:

I chose the profession of aerospace engineer. Because I love astronomy, science and technology.

#### B.	Discuss the current responsibilities of this profession:

Aerospace engineers design, develop and test aircraft, spacecraft, satellites and missiles. They use science, math and engineering skills to solve problems and perform their job. When I visited Cape Kennedy and observed SpaceX's projects I was very inspired.

Aerospace engineers use AI software to optimize structural components. For example GE used AI algorithms to redesign a 20 part fuel nozzle of the LEAP jet engine, into a single 3D printed component, reducing weight by 25%, all while increasing fuel efficiency.

First it is important that humans focus on what we can only understand, such as ethics. Also determining what is best for safety and application, "why is it being done and how will it be used?"

Overall, the essay demonstrates thoughtful engagement with an important topic and would be strengthened by a more focused discussion of a specific profession and a more balanced perspective on how humans and AI may work together in the future.

### Aerospace Engineer (6th Grade)

#### A.	Choose a job or profession that you may want to have in about 20 years:

I want to be an aerospace engineer.

#### B.	Discuss the current responsibilities of this profession:

Aerospace engineers design, overlook the making of, improve, etc. airplanes. There are several different types of aerospace engineering branches, such as aerodynamics, propulsion, structures/materials, and avionics/controls. Aerodynamics takes care of studying the movement of air around airfoils or fuselages. Propulsion designs or finds the systems needed to generate thrust for a specific airplane. Structures and materials finds and makes the best types of material used to withstand extreme conditions, while avionics and controls develop the code to make aerodynamically unstable fighters and rockets fly, make flight computers, plot flight patterns for airplanes, etc.

#### C. 	Search for one real example of how AI is currently being used in this profession, and discuss possible benefits and risks using AI in this way:

AI could help the avionics and control branch by making the best flight path in real time for airplanes depending on weather conditions, boundaries, air traffic, and more. Unlike pre-planned paths, AI could help save time by looking at the *changing* conditions while the airplane is in the air, not just the predicted conditions it would be going through at the time. This could decrease time to get from one place to another, as well as decreasing chances of holding patterns and such. Some downsides to this idea are that AI would only choose flight patterns that cut time and save fuel, not caring about how erratic or sophisticated they are, demanding skill from the pilots. AI could also miscalculate patterns, putting airplanes under more holding patterns and such than before.

#### D. 	Discuss how AI may change this profession over the next 20 years:

AI will, as patterns show now, take over at least the simplest tasks in the course of about 20 years. In the designing process, AI could provide suggestions about how to improve a current design to enhance structural integrity, or how to keep the airplane’s fuel consumption efficient while having the airplane’s radar visibility at its minimum. In the production process, it could automate the tools used to make the airplane to make the process efficient about the cycles, or find better ways to assemble different parts. AI could also make certain predictions about an airplane’s maintenance, based on its strength or period of time before the sensitive equipment wears down, so that imminent dangers could be identified and made sure that they are accounted for. 

#### E. 	As AI becomes capable of completing some tasks in this profession, discuss which human abilities may become more important to success:

Some human abilities aerospace engineers might need to improve on would be the ability to communicate, whether it is for colleagues or for AI to understand. Aerospace engineers would also have to improve on things like getting to the point of a conversation so people and AI would understand. AI summarizes data and looks for patterns, but with communication skills, you are able to tell AI what you want, or what kind of information you want AI to give you. Without them, AI acts almost human; they both cannot understand what you want, and usually try to guess, as for AI resulting in data or information you don’t think is right, which usually isn’t. You also have to look at the benefits communication skills give when talking to people, too. Aerospace engineering will still require people to run that profession, no matter how much AI advances. Before AI, communication was still important to aerospace engineering. People needed to make sure what they said was understood well. They needed to use communication skills not just to understand, they also used it to settle disagreements, have discussions to make decisions, and more. As AI use in aerospace engineering slowly increases, the need for communication will increase as well. 

#### F. 	Discuss what you can do now to develop these human abilities and prepare for this profession in a world where AI is widely used:

To develop communication skills, you can practice with your friends, family, even with AI. Practice means asking people questions, giving instructions, retelling events. All of these things help you get better at communicating to your colleagues, friends, family, and of course, AI. For example, if you are trying to convince your parents to buy a new bike, you have to persuade them to say yes, something that has to be done with organized thoughts, reasons that fit together, and they have to be good reasons for that. If you have jumbled thoughts jumping from one place to another, courtesy of bad communication skills, you won’t persuade them or maybe even make them understand your logic. To improve your communication skills, you could practice finding reasons for an essay until you find they are clear and concise to the reader. You need to also listen to others in order to communicate, ask questions that clear up ideas, collaborate well in a group, and more. For AI, you need to make it understand. But for people, you have to make them understand because you have clear thoughts, good questions, and the ability to work together.

#### Counselor Comments

This is a thoughtful and detailed submission that demonstrates a strong interest in aerospace engineering and a good understanding of how AI may influence the field. The discussion of different aerospace engineering specialties provides a broader perspective than many submissions, and the examples involving flight-path optimization, aircraft design, manufacturing, and predictive maintenance show careful consideration of both current and future applications of AI. The reflection also recognizes important human abilities, particularly communication, and provides several examples of why communication remains valuable even as AI becomes more capable. 

The discussion of communication in part (E) could be more focused on communication and collaboration among people, such as coordinating across engineering disciplines, making safety-critical decisions, and resolving disagreements within teams. In part (F), the examples of practicing communication are helpful, but the response could further explain specific communication skills that should be developed, such as active listening, organizing ideas clearly, asking effective questions, and adapting explanations to different audiences. 

Overall, the reflection demonstrates strong technical curiosity, thoughtful analysis of AI’s impact, and a mature understanding of the continuing importance of human skills in engineering.

### Pediatrician (5th Grade)

#### A.	Choose a job or profession that you may want to have in about 20 years.

I may want to become a pediatrician because they earn good money for 1 year in salary. (Average $180,000-$230,000.) Another reason is because it seems very fun to take care of cute little babies.

#### B.	Discuss the current responsibilities of this profession.

Some main responsibilities of being a pediatrician consist of:

- **Consulting with your patient:** This pretty much means they talk to you to know how you are feeling or if you have any infections, diseases, etc.
- **Caring about your patients:** This means you come to the doctor regularly to have checkups and they take care of you. They try their best to take care of you to see if you have a serious medical problem.
- **Preventive Care:** This means you give the patient a shot/vaccinations to make sure they don't have anything wrong.

#### C.	Search for one real example of how AI is currently being used in this profession, and discuss possible benefits and risk of using AI in this way.

**AI Detecting Heart Problems in Children**

"Researchers just applied learning AI Models to pediatric heart tracings recorded on watches, and the AI showed diagnostic performance on par with human experts." (Claude search, May 30, 2026) In other words, a watch using AI can help detect heart rhythm problems in children.

#### D.	Discuss how AI may change this profession over the next 20 years.

- **Earlier and more accurate Diagnosis:** Apps or wearables could monitor children's health continuously anywhere instantly.
- **Personalized Treatment:** Medications could be dosed more precisely for child safety. Which is currently a very big challenge.

#### E.	As AI becomes capable of completing some tasks in this profession, discuss which human abilities may become more important for success.

- **Communication Skills:** Building trust with patients and parents is key. It will become a big skill.
- **Collaboration:** No AI can navigate complex team dynamics or build human relationships.

#### F.	Discuss what you can do now to develop these human abilities and prepare for this profession in a world where AI is widely used.

- **Strengthen your communication skills:** Join a debate club, drama club, or a public speaking group.
- **Work on collaboration:** Practice giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.

#### Counselor Comments

This is a thoughtful and well-structured submission that addresses all parts of the assignment. The reflection clearly explains why the profession of pediatrician is appealing and demonstrates a good understanding of the responsibilities involved in caring for children. The example of AI-assisted detection of heart rhythm problems using wearable devices is particularly strong because it describes a real medical application of AI and explains how it can improve healthcare. The discussion of future developments, including continuous health monitoring and personalized treatment, shows good insight into how AI may influence medicine over the next 20 years. The responses also correctly identify communication and collaboration as important human abilities that will remain valuable, especially because pediatricians must build trust with both children and their parents.

Opportunities for improvement include discussing the potential risks of the AI application in part (c), such as false alarms, missed diagnoses, privacy concerns, or overreliance on automated recommendations. The explanation of the responsibilities of pediatricians could also be expanded to include diagnosing illnesses, interpreting medical tests, and coordinating care with other healthcare professionals. In part (e), the discussion of communication and collaboration is strong, but it could be further developed by explaining why empathy, compassion, and emotional understanding are especially important in pediatric medicine and difficult for AI to replace. 

Overall, this is a well-reasoned reflection that demonstrates a good understanding of both pediatric medicine and the growing role of AI in healthcare.

### Farming (5th Grade)

#### A.	Choose a job or profession that you may want to have in about 20 years.

In about 20 years, I think I would like my profession to be in farming. I would like to focus on wheat or corn farming because it feeds people and it's a peaceful profession.

#### B.	Discuss the current responsibilities of this profession.

The current responsibilities of someone in this profession are:

1. **Planting:** Today farmers use high tech GPS machines and tractors to evenly space seeds for maximum efficiency.
2. **Monitoring & Harvesting:** Farmers sometimes use high tech drones and cameras to make sure their crops are doing okay and ready to harvest when it's time to do so. Lots of farmers use Combine Harvesters for harvesting crops such as wheat, soybeans, and corn.
3. **Marketing & Selling:** Farming is a complex and risky business. These days it's hard to get farming products out to people. You need to plan, advertise, and sell and distribute to one or more reliable customer(s). Plus you need to understand politics and trade policy that can affect how much money you make.

#### C.	Search for one real example of how AI is currently being used in this profession, and discuss the possible benefits and risk of using AI in this way.

**Farming & AI:**

Farmers use AI-powered machinery and drones to plant crops, detect bad crops, and harvest corn and wheat automatically.

- **Benefits:** With AI-powered machinery farmers can grow more food to feed more people. Also, AI can help waste less water and fertilizer to save resources.
- **Risks:** Farmers and other people that help the farm could lose their jobs to AI machinery. Also, technology can break down overtime or make mistakes.

#### D.	Discuss how AI may change this profession over the next 20 years.

AI might change this profession by making farms look more advanced. There might be self-driving tractors to plant seeds with perfect precision all day and night. There also could be robots that automatically pick out all the bad crops.

#### E.	As AI becomes capable of completing some tasks in this profession, discuss which human abilities may become more important for success.

The ability to learn new AI systems and coordinate systems that plant, harvest, and sell the product. The ability to innovate and discover better and faster ways to farm will be important too.

#### F.	Discuss what you can do now to develop these human abilities and prepare for this profession in a world where AI is widely used.

I could pay more attention in STEM class, take AI online classes, and look more into the farming merit badge.

#### Counselor Comments

This is a well-organized and informative submission that demonstrates a good understanding of modern farming and the growing role of technology in agriculture. The discussion of planting, monitoring, harvesting, and marketing shows that farming involves much more than simply growing crops and reflects an appreciation of the business and technical aspects of the profession. The examples of GPS-guided equipment, drones, and AI-powered machinery are relevant and illustrate how technology is already being used in agriculture today. The reflection also identifies both benefits and risks of AI, including improved efficiency, reduced waste, potential job displacement, and the possibility of equipment failures. The discussion of future developments, such as self-driving tractors and automated crop inspection, shows thoughtful consideration of how farming may evolve over the next 20 years. 

Opportunities for improvement include providing a more specific real-world example of an AI system currently used in farming, rather than describing AI applications in general. The discussion of human abilities could also be expanded beyond learning new technology to include decision-making, problem-solving, adaptability, and communication, since farmers must often respond to changing weather, market conditions, and unexpected challenges. The final response would be stronger with more specific examples of how these abilities can be developed, such as participating in agricultural programs, learning data analysis, or gaining hands-on farming experience. 

Overall, this is a thoughtful reflection that demonstrates a strong understanding of both farming and the potential impact of AI on agriculture.
